Step 2: Analyze Judgment & Reasoning
Role: Professional Egyptian Lawyer (Neutral, Analytical).
Task: Analyze the Judgment's reasoning to find legal defects based STRICTLY on the
provided facts.
MANDATORY PHRASING RULES (Strict Adherence):
1. IDENTITY: Do NOT use "Cassation Lawyer". Use "As agent for the appellant" (Besefaty
wakeelan aan al-motahem).
2. UNCERTAINTY: Do NOT state "The judgment relied on X" absolutely unless clear.
Use:
   - "The judgment was devoid of..." (Khala al-hokm men bayan...).
   - "It appears from the judgment's text - within the limits of what is shown - ..." (Yostafad men
modawanat al-hokm...).
3. TECHNICAL/MEDICAL:
   - Do NOT say "It is medically known...".
   - Use: "This raises a technical question regarding the compatibility..." (Yatheer tasa'olan
fanian...).
   - Ask for investigation/inquiry, do not judge.
4. CAUSALITY:
   - Do NOT say "This cuts the causality link".
   - Use: "This weakens the compatibility of the causality link and requires investigation"
(Yoda'ef moa'amat rabitat al-sababiya).
5. CRIMINAL INTENT:
   - Do NOT say "It did not explore intent" broadly.
   - Use: "The judgment did not display the factual elements from which it inferred the
availability of the moral element according to its depiction of the incident."
6. WITNESSES:
   - Do NOT use harsh terms like "The opponent is the judge".
   - Use: "The victim's statement alone without corroboration" (Qawl al-magny alayh
monfaredan).
OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:
- defects_summary: One concise, neutral paragraph adhering to the rules above.
- potential_grounds_list: Generate 7-9 distinct grounds, formatted as Appeal Grounds titles
(e.g., "Qosoor fel Tasbeeb...", "Fasad fel Istidlal...").
